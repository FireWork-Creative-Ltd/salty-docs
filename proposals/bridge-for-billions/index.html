Destination

Why Morocco works
for your team

  • Accessible from 3 continents — Direct flights from Madrid, Paris, London, Amsterdam, and Nairobi via Casablanca. Short connections from Latin America through European hubs.
  • Exceptional value — Luxury-tier venues and catering at a fraction of Western European pricing.
  • Rich cultural backdrop — Moroccan architecture, cuisine, and coastline create a memorable setting that elevates a working trip into a shared experience.
  • Strong corporate infrastructure — Morocco's top hotels have experienced events teams accustomed to groups of 50+.
  • Visa-friendly — No visa required for EU, US, Canadian, and Kenyan passport holders.

Option A SALTY's Suggested Destination

Casablanca

Connected and cosmopolitan · Morocco's international hub · Urban energy

Why Casablanca

  • Home to Mohammed V International (CMN) — Morocco's largest and best-connected airport with direct flights from Madrid, Paris, London, Nairobi, and dozens more
  • For a team flying from three continents, this is the simplest logistics — fewer connections, fewer delays, fewer missed arrivals
  • Morocco's commercial capital with cosmopolitan energy — restaurants, culture, and nightlife for free evenings
  • Shorter transfer times from airport to hotel (30–45 minutes)

Flight Access

  • Mohammed V International (CMN) — direct from Madrid, Paris, London, Amsterdam, Nairobi + extensive global connections
  • Best option for minimizing layovers and travel complexity across your entire team

Example Property We're Evaluating

Villa Sahrai — Boutique design hotel, 56 rooms, 100m from the ocean. Intimate atmosphere with strong concierge service. Meeting space is functional though not purpose-built for groups of 50.

Additional Properties Being Sourced

We are finalizing quotes from several additional Casablanca properties with stronger dedicated meeting infrastructure and capacity for your full group.

Casablanca is the strongest choice for flight logistics — and for a distributed team across three continents, that matters. We're confident in the venue options being sourced.
Estimated ground cost: €38,000–45,000

Option B

Agadir

Coastal retreat setting · Atlantic coastline · Resort infrastructure

Why Agadir

  • Purpose-built resort town on Morocco's southern Atlantic coast — designed for exactly this kind of experience
  • Hotels with dedicated conference infrastructure: multiple meeting rooms, AV packages, breakout spaces, and Day Delegate rates
  • Beach access and resort amenities give your team natural decompression between workshop days
  • A distinctly different environment from daily routines — the change of setting is part of the value

Flight Access

  • Agadir Al Massira Airport (AGA) — direct flights from Paris, London, Amsterdam, Brussels
  • Madrid connections available, though most require 1 stop
  • Fewer direct routes than Casablanca — this is the primary reason we suggest Casablanca for a team distributed across three continents

Example Property We're Evaluating

Sofitel Agadir Royal Bay Resort — 5-star, 273 rooms, 6 meeting rooms, Day Delegate package available, airport shuttle included. Strong fit for a workshop-intensive format with 50 people.

Agadir offers the strongest retreat atmosphere and the most predictable meeting infrastructure. The tradeoff is flight logistics — your Nairobi and Latin American team members will likely need at least one connection.
Estimated ground cost: €42,000–45,000
